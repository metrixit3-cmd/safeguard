Know your platform

One account. Protection shaped around your phone.

Today's companion reports permissioned GPS, Theft Mode and foreground alerts. Native device-owner protections need a dedicated managed-device integration.

Android

The companion sends GPS reports over HTTPS and relays Theft Mode alerts while the page is active. USB-flash prevention, bootloader protection, reset guards and persistent tracking require a native Android Enterprise/device-owner integration.

iPhone

The companion shares GPS and displays Theft Mode alerts with your permission while it is open. iOS web pages cannot lock the phone, override silent mode, keep tracking after power-off, or invoke Apple MDM actions.

Good questions

Clear answers about how it works.

Does SAFEGUARD provide real-time GPS tracking?

Yes. After pairing, granting location permission, and starting GPS sharing, the phone companion sends its latest browser location about every three seconds while the page is open and connected. This is foreground browser tracking: mobile browsers can suspend it when the page is backgrounded, the browser closes, the phone is offline, or the phone powers off. SAFEGUARD cannot retrieve a new location in those states.

Can SAFEGUARD remotely lock my phone or sound an alarm?

Theft Mode is saved to your account and checked by the paired companion while its page is open and connected. It can show a red on-screen alert and play a siren after you enable audio on that phone. It cannot lock the Android or iOS operating system, override silent mode, or guarantee an alarm while the page is suspended or offline. System-level remote lock requires the platform’s native lost-device or enrolled management service.

Does SAFEGUARD detect SIM tampering?

No. SIM-change detection is not part of the current browser companion. It requires platform-specific native code, permissions and supported device integrations; it is not available on every Android or iPhone.

Does SAFEGUARD support Android Enterprise or MDM?

No. Android Enterprise and Apple MDM are not integrated with this release. They require separate management services, native enrollment, and eligible devices. A browser page does not receive those administrator privileges.

How is account access protected?

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and browser account sessions use signed, HTTP-only cookies. Phone pairing uses a device-scoped bearer token stored in that phone browser’s local storage. GPS coordinates are sensitive personal data stored with the device record; the application does not encrypt those coordinates itself at rest. Use HTTPS and secure the MongoDB host and its storage appropriately.

What happens if the phone is offline?

The dashboard retains the last GPS report and its timestamp. It cannot receive a new location or deliver a Theft Mode change until the companion reconnects. Browser GPS sharing does not work while the phone is powered off. Apple Find My and Google Find Hub have separate offline-finding features on supported, preconfigured devices; they are not controlled by SAFEGUARD.

Is the 30-day free trial active?

Yes. A 30-day trial starts automatically when you create an account. Paid pricing and billing are not yet available.

Can SAFEGUARD prevent every factory reset or uninstall?

No. This web application cannot prevent shutdown, uninstall, software flashing, or factory reset. Android factory-reset restrictions and Apple device lock commands require eligible devices enrolled in the platform’s management program; those integrations are not included here.
